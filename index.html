How to use

Drag the fridge or the garden trough to move them. Tick “Move built-ins” to unlock counters, sink, range, bath fixtures and the rest. Select any piece to get a rotate handle (tap for 90°, drag for any angle) and, on furniture, a green corner dot to resize. Pick a numbered layout from the list to load it, or add pieces from the box. The shaded band is the back of a piece and the small arrow shows which way it faces. Everything snaps to quarter feet. R rotates, Delete removes or puts back.

      Good to know

      From the scan, the walkthrough and Amit.

      • Ceilings are 9′1″ in the kitchen, hall and bath, and up to 9′11″ in the main room.
      • The bay is a raised, painted ledge at sill height, not floor. Seats go on the floor in front of it, facing the window.
      • Gas range and electric baseboard heat.
      • Water and garbage are included. You pay gas, electric and internet.
      • Internet: Amit suggests Sonic or Monkey Brains (a local provider).
      • The roof deck is reached through the kitchen window. The landlord cleans the runoff once a year.
      • The ironing board folds up into a built-in cupboard in the kitchen.
      • Bins are downstairs. Trucks collect on the side street about twice a week.
      • The fire escape screen has to come off to use it. No plants out there.

      Walls, doors and windows come from the phone scan, so they should be good to a few inches. Still drawn by eye because the scans did not capture them: the depth of the bay, the range, fridge, heaters and bath fixtures.
