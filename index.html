The plan

Drag the fridge, the eat-in area or the planter to move them. Tick “Move built-ins” to unlock counters, sink, range, bath fixtures and the rest. Select any piece to get a rotate handle (tap for 90°, drag for any angle) and, on furniture, a green corner dot to resize. Press “Suggest a layout” to place all your furniture, or add pieces from the box. Everything snaps to quarter feet. R rotates, Delete removes or puts back.
